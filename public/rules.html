How to play

Five to ten of you sit around a table. A few of you are spies; the rest are operatives of the resistance. The spies know each other. Nobody else knows anything. Over five missions the operatives try to complete three; the spies try to sink three. Talk as much as you like, about anything, at any time.

Setup

Everyone gets a secret card. The spies then get a moment to recognise each other (on this site: your card shows their names). A random player is the first leader.

A round

  1. The leader proposes a team. Exactly as many players as the mission needs (table below), and the leader may include themself.
  2. Everyone votes on that team, all at once, then the votes are shown. A majority approves it. A tie rejects it. If it is rejected, the player to the leader's left becomes leader and proposes a new team.
  3. The team goes on the mission. Each member secretly plays a Success or a Fail card. Operatives must play Success; spies may play either. The cards are shuffled and revealed. One Fail sinks the mission.

Five rejected teams in one round and the spies win on the spot. A rejection is never free.

Winning

The game ends the moment either side has three missions: three successes and the operatives win; three fails and the spies win. The spies also win if five teams are rejected in a single round.

Advice

  • Operatives: get on teams. Watch who approves what, and who was on which mission. A team that came back clean is worth reusing.
  • Spies: act like an operative. Vote the way an operative would. Failing the very first mission is the fastest way to be found out.
  • Everyone: a rejected team costs one of five. Use them, but not all of them.

Options on this site

Blind spies skips the reveal: the spies do not know each other either.

玩法說明

五到十個人圍著一桌。其中幾個是間諜，其他人是反抗軍的成員。間諜彼此認識，其他人什麼都不知道。五次任務裡，反抗軍要完成三次，間諜要搞砸三次。你可以隨時說任何話。

準備

每個人拿到一張祕密身分牌。接著間諜互相認一下（在這個網站上，你的牌會直接列出同夥的名字）。隨機選一個人當第一任隊長。

一個回合

  1. 隊長提名隊伍。人數要剛好等於這次任務需要的人數（見下表），隊長可以把自己放進去。
  2. 所有人投票，同時亮票。過半數贊成就通過；平手算否決。被否決的話，隊長換給左手邊的人，重新提名。
  3. 隊伍執行任務。每個隊員祕密出一張「成功」或「失敗」。反抗軍只能出成功，間諜兩種都可以。牌洗過後翻開，只要有一張失敗，任務就失敗。

同一回合連續五次提名都被否決，間諜直接獲勝。否決不是免費的。

勝負

任一方累積三次任務，遊戲立刻結束：三次成功，反抗軍贏；三次失敗，間諜贏。同一回合五次提名被否決，也算間諜贏。

建議

  • 反抗軍：想辦法上隊。記住誰贊成了什麼、誰上過哪次任務。乾淨回來的隊伍值得再派一次。
  • 間諜：表現得像反抗軍。投票要投得像反抗軍。第一次任務就搞砸，是最快被抓到的方法。
  • 所有人：一次否決就用掉五次裡的一次。可以用，但別全用光。

網站上的選項

盲間諜：跳過認人的步驟，間諜也不知道彼此是誰。
